Expert Financial Statement Guide

Converting bank statement PDFs into structured Excel sheets requires maintaining ledger integrity. Here is how to configure and clean your extracted data efficiently:

  1. 1. Standardize Coordinates Once you click "Convert & Preview", our engine dynamically tracks column coordinates based on the table headers (`DATE`, `DESCRIPTION`, etc.) to prevent structural column shifting.
  2. 2. Clean Duplicate Headers Multi-page statements repeat the table headers on every new page. Click the 🧹 Remove Repeating Headers rule in the workspace panel to delete these duplicate lines instantly.
  3. 3. Filter Extraneous Metadata Document headers, opening messages, and terms of service don't belong in your final ledger. Click the **⚡ Auto-Filter Noise Rows** button to automatically wipe out non-transaction lines based on date-patterns.
  4. 4. Double-Check the Balance Matrix Our built-in calculation engine mathematically verifies each transaction row. If you correct any typos in the withdrawal or deposit cells, the balances recalculate in real-time.
