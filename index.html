About the Studio

GCP Automation Studio optimizes your financial workflows. Integrated directly into your Google Workspace™ environment, our tools empower users to seamlessly organize, analyze, and process statement documents, receipts, and invoices without leaving their cloud environment.

Application Setup Guide

Follow these instructions to configure our extensions after installation:

  1. 1. Install the App Click the "Install" button on the Google Workspace Marketplace™ listing page.
  2. 2. Grant Permissions Review and accept the OAuth authorization prompts to securely connect your workspace pipelines.
  3. 3. Initialize Integration Open your Google Workspace™ container (such as Google Sheets™ or the sidebar layout), find the processor icon, and start scanning your financial layouts instantly.
